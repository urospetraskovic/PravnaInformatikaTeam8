LEGAL FRAMEWORK - MONTENEGRIN CRIMINAL CODE ARTICLES
================================================================================

Common Articles for Mobbing/Harassment Charges:

Article 228 (KZ) - "Threatening or Humiliating"
- Covers acts that threaten, humiliate, or demean a person
- Frequently used in workplace harassment cases

Article 229 (KZ) - "Harassment, Threats, Intimidation"
- Covers systematic harassment or threats
- Can include repeated unwanted contact

Article 131 (KZ) - "Persecution/Persecution by Officials"
- Covers systematic persecution
- Can apply to abuse of power in workplace context

Article 289-297 (KZ) - "Bodily Injuries"
- May be charged if harassment led to physical harm
- Range from light to serious injuries

Article 55 (General Provisions) - "Mitigating/Aggravating Circumstances"
- Modifies sentences based on circumstances
- Often related to employment relationship

ATTRIBUTES FOR CBR SYSTEM (Case-Based Reasoning)
================================================================================

Based on these cases, we will define attributes such as:

1. Perpetrator Type (employer, supervisor, colleague, multiple)
2. Victim Type (employee, subordinate, vulnerable person)
3. Harassment Type (verbal abuse, threats, discrimination, physical, psychological)
4. Duration (single incident, repeated over period)
5. Workplace Environment (private sector, public sector, state institution)
6. Power Dynamics (superior-subordinate, peer-to-peer, mob harassment)
7. Witnesses (yes/no, how many)
8. Documented Evidence (yes/no, type)
9. Prior Incidents (first offense, repeat offender)
10. Physical Harm (none, minor, serious)
11. Mental/Psychological Harm (mild, moderate, severe)
12. Articles Violated (primary, secondary)
13. Sentence Type (fine, imprisonment, conditional)
14. Sentence Duration (if applicable)
15. Aggravating Circumstances (abuse of authority, particularly vulnerable victim, etc.)
